# Credit Risk Scoring & Customer Default Prediction
Data insights to assess credit risk and predict customer default behavior

## Key Aspects of the Project
- Credit risk profiling and default prediction
- Identification of key demographic and financial risk drivers
- Binary classification of good vs bad customers
- Emphasis on model interpretability for risk decisions

## Methodology
- Data cleaning and feature engineering
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Handling imbalanced classes using resampling techniques
- Predictive modeling with classification algorithms
- Model evaluation and feature importance analysis

## Tools
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Scikit-learn
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Stakeholder-Centric Analysis
- Supports credit approval and risk management teams
- Enables proactive monitoring of high-risk customers
- Aligns analytical outputs with regulatory and business needs

## Ethical Considerations
- Responsible handling of sensitive financial data
- Avoidance of biased assumptions in credit decisions
- Focus on transparency and fairness

## Deliverables
- Credit risk modeling notebooks
- Risk driver analysis
- Business recommendations for lending decisions
- Model deployment 👉🏻 https://creditrisk-model.streamlit.app
